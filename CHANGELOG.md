# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Released 2026-11-25. Requires Minecraft: Bedrock Edition 26.60 or newer.

### New

- Vein Miner: mines the connected vein of the ore you break (face, edge and corner contact; regular,
  deepslate and lit variants together), up to a limit set in the pack settings (default 64 blocks, counting
  the first). Sneak to mine one block. Vanilla drops, Fortune, Silk Touch, tool tier, Unbreaking, mining XP
  and exhaustion for every block; stops before the pickaxe would break.
- Ore Smelter: smelts raw iron, copper and gold and ancient debris as they drop, with furnace XP. Silk Touch
  takes priority.
- Enchanted books for both in the chests of 15 structure loot tables, sharing the vanilla enchanted book
  entry so no other loot changes.
- Anvil menu to apply the books, with vanilla-style pricing and anvil wear, and a confirmation step.
- Records kept on the pickaxe as item data and a gray lore line, each restoring the other; removed by the
  grindstone.
